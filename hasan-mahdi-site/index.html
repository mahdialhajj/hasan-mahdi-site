About

Hasan Mahdi is a photographer based between Beirut and the Bekaa. He started in 2014 photographing stonework and restoration sites, and the habit stuck: return to a place often enough and it starts to show you something.

His work has run in regional and international press, and he shoots commissions for architects, hotels, cultural institutions and private clients. Everything is edited by hand, and archive files are kept for ten years so a client can come back for a frame long after the job closes.
